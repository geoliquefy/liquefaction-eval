## 3. Findings

### MAJOR

**M1. Deviation 3 changes most of one printed cell.** On Hyogoken-Nambu, 22 of 26 P1 cases (30 of 36 P2) fall on
empty Zhu grid cells and are read as 0. The empty cells hold 15 of 19 "yes" cases and all 7 "no" cases. On the cases
with a real grid value there is no "no" case. So the printed Zhu AUC 0.605 (P2 0.615) comes from 4 non-zero "yes"
values against 22 tied zeros. The page prints "Zhu grids: Hyogoken-Nambu 36 cases" and not the 30 empty cells.
W2 prints NOT ALLOWED, so no pooled line moves.
Smallest fix: in the P1 and P2 tables, print the count beside that cell: "0.605 (22 of 26 cases off the model grid,
read as 0)". Reword deviation 3 to say it sets 85% of one cell's inputs. Do not re-score.

**M2. The Kobe shaking-only AUC 0.158 is not a units error, but its PGA source is not stated.** PGA on the 26 P1
cases is 0.32 to 0.66 g, so the units are right. In 25 of 26 cases the NGL tag says "site response analysis". "Yes"
sites have a median of 0.47 g and "no" sites 0.58 g. A PGA from a site response analysis can be lower at softened
sites, so it may not be independent of the outcome. This review does not test that. The same PGA goes into Level 0,
LSN and LPI. The W1 P1 mean of +0.184 is +0.108 with Kobe left out. The card prints the leave-one-out range but
does not name Kobe. No registered gate checks a plausibility range for AUC, so this was not a gate miss.
Smallest fix: add a note on the Kobe rows and on W1: "PGA tag: site response analysis (25 of 26); shaking-only AUC
below 0.5; W1 without Kobe +0.108". Keep Kobe in the population, because removing it now would follow the results.

**M3. The world tables print the B&I exposure only.** Registration section 5 and 3c section 6 item 7 also need the
Zhu and LSN-global marks. The JSON holds them, but the page does not show them. The missing marks:
- Zhu 2017: EVENT IN TRAINING on Hyogoken-Nambu and Chi-Chi.
- LSN global fragility: SEEN (same compilation) on Emilia, Tangshan and Westmorland. 3c says to print it as "in
  sample (fit)".

The row name "BI14-LSN (both LSN fragility rows)" next to "UNSEEN 46" on Emilia can make a reader think that the
LSN-global row is unseen there.
Smallest fix: the renderer adds two exposure columns (Zhu, LSN global) from `world.populations.*.exposure`.
